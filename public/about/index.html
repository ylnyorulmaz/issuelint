02 / THE APPROACH

Two levels of review.
One clear next step.

01FAST FIRST PASS

Quick Scan

One lightweight Gemini Decision model request checks eleven dimensions and returns an easy-to-read green tick or red cross for each.

No long explanations. No forced template. A green tick means the model did not flag an issue in that dimension—not that the task is guaranteed correct.

02WHEN YOU NEED MORE

Analyze v2

Go deeper with a selective, independent GPT-OSS review of dimensions identified by the initial screen. Read specific reasons, source excerpts and questions to clarify.

The deeper review takes longer. Model judgments are suggestions to evaluate, not a substitute for the team's decisions.

04 / WHO IT'S FOR

For people who turn
requests into real work.

IssueLint is designed for product managers, engineering leads, developers, QA teams, agency teams and independent builders—as well as anyone who needs to hand off work with fewer assumptions.

You can paste text from Jira, GitHub Issues, Linear, a document or a chat. IssueLint is tool-agnostic; it doesn't need access to your project-management account, and it doesn't write changes back to those tools.

05 / COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things worth knowing.

Can I use IssueLint with just one paragraph?

Yes. The title and additional fields are optional in the browser. A plain-text task description is enough to run a review during the private AI preview.

Does a green Quick Scan result mean my task is perfect?

No. Quick Scan is a preliminary model classification. It can miss problems or mark dimensions as not applicable. The full review adds independent scrutiny, but neither mode guarantees correctness.

Does IssueLint connect to Jira, GitHub or Linear?

Not currently. Copy and paste a task from any of those tools; IssueLint reviews the text without connecting to or editing the underlying ticket.

Is IssueLint open for everyone?

The site is public, but live AI analysis currently requires a private preview access code. Public accounts and unrestricted AI access have not launched.
